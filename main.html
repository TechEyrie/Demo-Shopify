Where Industrial Engineering Meets Childlike Wonder

V2 3D Printing was founded in 2023 by two former aerospace CAD designers who grew tired of disposable, brittle injection-molded plastics. We believed kinetic toys should be sturdy enough for everyday adventures yet engineered with the microscopic precision of high-end chronometers.

Today, our micro-factory runs continuous multi-filament testing, calibrating layer line flow down to microns to achieve silky smooth transitions and frictionless joint rotation directly off the build sheet.
